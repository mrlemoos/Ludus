# Terms of service

Version: 2026-11-01

The consumer-facing terms are published at `/terms` and localised in British English, European Spanish and European French. Keep this source aligned with [Policy.tsx](../src/Policy.tsx).

## Chess and coaching

Ludus is an adults-only chess service. Browser Stockfish analysis is free and unlimited. An AI coach review uses one credit only when generation succeeds. One introductory AI review is available per account for life. Beta accounts receive 30 monthly credits until their original beta expiry. Only accounts with an explicit permanent complimentary grant receive 30 monthly credits after beta. Monthly credits expire at month end and are used before paid credits.

A paid pack contains ten AI coach reviews for EUR 4.99. Paid credits do not expire. Opening or replaying saved text and changing interface language do not use credits or regenerate a review. Each saved review allows five successful follow-up answers. Failed AI generations and answers do not count. Saved reviews and answers remain available.

## Payments and refunds

Stripe hosts Checkout. Credits are granted only after Ludus verifies a paid webhook. Purchases remain unavailable while billing, tax and account checks are incomplete.

Unused paid packs are refunded on request. Refunds are processed manually. Requests for partly used packs receive individual manual review. Mandatory consumer rights are preserved. An open payment dispute freezes unused credits from the affected pack while it is reviewed.

The manual refund request channel must be published before charging is enabled. Do not invent or infer a support email address.
